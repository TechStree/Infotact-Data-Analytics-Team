# Discount and Sales Correlation Analysis

## Correlation Result

Correlation between Discount and Units Sold:

0.0026

## Interpretation

* The correlation value is extremely close to zero.
* There is almost no linear relationship between discounts and sales volume.
* Increasing discounts does not appear to significantly increase sales within this dataset.

## Business Insights

* Discount strategies alone may not be sufficient to drive higher sales.
* Customer purchasing behavior may depend on multiple factors beyond discounts.
* Inventory levels, seasonality, promotions, weather conditions, and product categories may play a larger role.
* Future forecasting models should consider multiple variables instead of focusing only on discount levels.

## Conclusion

Discount and Units Sold show almost no correlation in this dataset. This suggests that discount levels are not the primary factor influencing sales performance.
