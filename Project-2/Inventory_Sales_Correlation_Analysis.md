# Inventory and Sales Correlation Analysis

## Correlation Result

Correlation between Inventory Level and Units Sold:

0.5900

## Interpretation

* The correlation value is positive.
* A value of 0.5900 indicates a moderate positive relationship.
* Products with higher inventory levels generally tend to record higher sales.
* The relationship is not strong enough to suggest that inventory alone determines sales performance.

## Business Insights

* Inventory availability influences sales performance.
* Maintaining sufficient stock can help meet customer demand.
* Other variables such as price, promotions, seasonality, and weather conditions also impact sales.
* Inventory planning should consider multiple business factors rather than inventory levels alone.

## Conclusion

Inventory Level and Units Sold show a moderate positive relationship. This finding supports the importance of inventory management in supply chain operations and demand forecasting.
