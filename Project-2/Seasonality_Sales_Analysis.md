# Seasonality Sales Analysis

## Average Units Sold by Season

| Season | Average Units Sold |
| ------ | -----------------: |
| Autumn |             137.78 |
| Winter |             136.83 |
| Spring |             135.83 |
| Summer |             135.43 |

## Findings

* Autumn records the highest average units sold.
* Summer records the lowest average units sold.
* Seasonal differences exist but are relatively moderate.
* Customer demand changes slightly across different seasons.

## Business Insights

* Seasonal trends can influence inventory requirements.
* Products may experience higher demand during Autumn and Winter periods.
* Seasonal information can improve forecasting accuracy.
* Supply chain planning should account for seasonal demand fluctuations.

## Conclusion

Seasonality has a measurable impact on sales performance. Including seasonal information in forecasting models can improve demand prediction and inventory management decisions.
