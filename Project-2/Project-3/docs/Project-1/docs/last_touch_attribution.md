# Last Touch Attribution

## What is Last Touch Attribution?

Last Touch Attribution is a marketing attribution model that assigns 100% of the conversion credit to the last marketing channel a customer interacted with before making a purchase or completing a desired action.

## How It Works

The final interaction before the conversion receives all the credit, regardless of how many previous marketing channels influenced the customer.

## Example

Customer Journey:

Facebook Ad → Email Campaign → Google Search → Purchase

Credit:

- Facebook Ad = 0%
- Email Campaign = 0%
- Google Search = 100%

## Advantages

- Easy to understand and implement.
- Identifies the channel that directly leads to conversion.
- Useful for measuring the effectiveness of final conversion channels.

## Disadvantages

- Ignores earlier customer interactions.
- Does not show the complete customer journey.
- May overvalue the last marketing channel.

## Business Use Case

An online shopping company wants to identify which channel generates the most final sales. Using Last Touch Attribution, it can determine whether Google Ads, Email Marketing, or Social Media drives the final conversion.

## Conclusion

Last Touch Attribution is useful for understanding which channel closes the sale. However, it should be used with other attribution models to understand the complete customer journey.
