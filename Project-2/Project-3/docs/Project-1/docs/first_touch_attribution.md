# First Touch Attribution

## What is First Touch Attribution?

First Touch Attribution is a marketing attribution model that gives 100% credit for a conversion to the first marketing channel through which a customer first interacted with a business.

## How It Works

When a customer first visits a website through a marketing channel (such as Facebook, Google Ads, or Email), that first interaction receives all the credit for the final purchase or conversion.

## Example

Customer Journey:

Facebook Ad → Website Visit → Email → Google Search → Purchase

Credit:

Facebook Ad = 100%

Email = 0%

Google Search = 0%

## Advantages

- Identifies the channel that creates first customer awareness.
- Easy to understand and implement.
- Useful for measuring brand awareness campaigns.

## Disadvantages

- Ignores all later interactions.
- Does not represent the complete customer journey.
- May underestimate the impact of other marketing channels.

## Business Use Case

A company wants to know which marketing channel brings the highest number of new customers. Using the First Touch Attribution model, it can identify whether Facebook Ads, Google Ads, or another channel is introducing the most customers.

## Conclusion

First Touch Attribution is best suited for businesses that want to measure customer acquisition and brand awareness. However, it should be combined with other attribution models for a more complete understanding of the customer journey.
