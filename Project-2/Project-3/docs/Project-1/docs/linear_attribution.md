# Linear Attribution

## What is Linear Attribution?

Linear Attribution is a marketing attribution model that distributes conversion credit equally across all customer touchpoints. Every interaction in the customer journey receives the same amount of credit.

## How It Works

If a customer interacts with multiple marketing channels before making a purchase, each channel receives an equal share of the conversion credit.

## Example

Customer Journey:

Facebook Ad → Email Campaign → Google Search → Purchase

Credit:

- Facebook Ad = 33.3%
- Email Campaign = 33.3%
- Google Search = 33.3%

## Advantages

- Considers the complete customer journey.
- Gives equal importance to every marketing channel.
- Easy to understand and compare with other attribution models.

## Disadvantages

- Assumes every touchpoint contributes equally.
- May not accurately represent the true impact of each marketing channel.

## Business Use Case

A company wants to understand the contribution of every marketing channel involved in the customer journey. Linear Attribution helps evaluate the overall performance of all channels instead of focusing only on the first or last interaction.

## Comparison

| Model | Credit Given To |
|--------|-----------------|
| First Touch | First interaction |
| Last Touch | Last interaction |
| Linear | All interactions equally |

## Conclusion

Linear Attribution provides a balanced view of customer interactions and is useful when businesses want to evaluate the contribution of every marketing channel.
