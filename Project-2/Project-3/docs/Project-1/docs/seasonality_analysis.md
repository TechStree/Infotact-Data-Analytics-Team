# Seasonality Analysis

## What is Seasonality?

Seasonality is a repeating pattern in data that occurs at regular intervals, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. It is caused by recurring events like holidays, weather changes, festivals, or business cycles.

## Characteristics of Seasonality

- Repeats at fixed time intervals.
- Can be influenced by holidays and festivals.
- Helps identify recurring demand patterns.
- Useful for planning inventory and resources.

## Examples

### Example 1: Retail Sales
Sales increase during festivals like Diwali and Christmas due to higher customer demand.

### Example 2: Ice Cream Sales
Ice cream sales are higher during summer and lower during winter.

### Example 3: Tourism
Tourist visits increase during vacation seasons and decrease during off-seasons.

## Difference Between Trend and Seasonality

| Trend | Seasonality |
|--------|-------------|
| Long-term movement in data | Short-term repeating pattern |
| May increase or decrease over time | Repeats at regular intervals |
| Shows overall business growth | Shows periodic changes |

## Importance of Seasonality Analysis

- Improves demand forecasting.
- Helps manage inventory.
- Supports production planning.
- Optimizes marketing campaigns.
- Reduces stock shortages and excess inventory.

## Business Use Case

An e-commerce company analyzes seasonal shopping trends. It notices that electronics sales increase significantly during festive seasons, so it increases inventory before the festival begins.

## Conclusion

Seasonality analysis helps businesses understand recurring patterns in data, making demand forecasting and business planning more accurate.
