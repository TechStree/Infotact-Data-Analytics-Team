# Trend Analysis

## What is Trend?

A trend is the long-term direction or movement of data over a period of time. It shows whether values are increasing, decreasing, or remaining stable.

## Types of Trend

### 1. Upward Trend
An upward trend occurs when data values increase over time.

Example:
Monthly Sales:
January = 500
February = 650
March = 800

### 2. Downward Trend
A downward trend occurs when data values decrease over time.

Example:
January = 900
February = 750
March = 600

### 3. No Trend
When data does not show a clear increasing or decreasing pattern.

## Importance of Trend Analysis

- Helps forecast future demand.
- Supports inventory planning.
- Assists in business decision-making.
- Identifies long-term business growth.

## Business Use Case

A retail company analyzes the sales trend of a product over the last two years. If sales are increasing steadily, the company can stock more inventory to meet future demand.

## Conclusion

Trend analysis helps businesses understand long-term patterns in data and supports accurate forecasting and planning.
