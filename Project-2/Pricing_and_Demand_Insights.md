# Pricing and Demand Insights

## Overview

This report summarizes the relationship between product pricing, competitor pricing, and forecasted demand within the supply chain dataset.

## Demand Forecast Findings

* Average Demand Forecast: 141.49 units
* Maximum Forecast: 518.55 units
* Minimum Forecast: -9.99 units
* Demand values vary significantly across products and stores.

## Product Pricing Findings

* Average Product Price: 55.14
* Minimum Price: 10.00
* Maximum Price: 100.00
* Product prices cover low, medium, and high price ranges.

## Competitor Pricing Findings

* Average Competitor Price: 55.15
* Minimum Competitor Price: 5.03
* Maximum Competitor Price: 104.94
* Competitor pricing closely matches product pricing on average.

## Business Insights

* Pricing plays a major role in influencing customer demand.
* Competitor pricing may impact purchasing decisions and sales performance.
* Products with higher forecasted demand should be monitored carefully for inventory planning.
* Negative demand forecast values should be investigated during data preprocessing.
* Both product price and competitor price can be used as forecasting features.

## Conclusion

Demand forecasting, product pricing, and competitor pricing are strongly connected business factors. Understanding these relationships will help improve inventory planning, forecasting accuracy, and supply chain decision-making.
