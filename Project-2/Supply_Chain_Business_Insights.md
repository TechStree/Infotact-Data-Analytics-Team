# Supply Chain Business Insights

## Overview

The retail inventory dataset contains 73,100 records across multiple stores, products, regions, seasons, and weather conditions. The dataset is well-balanced and suitable for supply chain analytics, demand forecasting, and anomaly detection.

## Key Findings

### Data Quality

* No missing values were found.
* No duplicate records were found.
* The dataset is complete and ready for analysis.

### Product Categories

* All product categories are evenly distributed.
* Furniture has the highest number of records.
* Electronics has the lowest number of records.

### Regional Distribution

* East region contains the highest number of records.
* West region contains the lowest number of records.
* Regional representation is balanced.

### Seasonality

* Seasonal records are evenly distributed across Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter.
* Seasonality can be used as an important forecasting feature.

### Weather Conditions

* Sunny, Rainy, Cloudy, and Snowy conditions are almost equally represented.
* Weather can influence customer demand and purchasing behavior.

### Holiday and Promotion Impact

* Promotional and non-promotional periods are evenly distributed.
* Holiday and promotion information can help explain changes in demand.

### Inventory and Sales

* Average Inventory Level: 274.47 units
* Average Units Sold: 136.46 units
* Inventory levels are generally higher than sales volumes.
* Some products recorded zero sales and may require further investigation.

## Conclusion

The dataset provides strong coverage across products, regions, seasons, weather conditions, and promotional periods. These features make it highly suitable for demand forecasting, anomaly detection, and supply chain optimization.
