# Weather Sales Analysis

## Average Units Sold by Weather Condition

| Weather Condition | Average Units Sold |
| ----------------- | -----------------: |
| Sunny             |             138.03 |
| Cloudy            |             136.76 |
| Snowy             |             135.91 |
| Rainy             |             135.16 |

## Findings

* Sunny weather records the highest average units sold.
* Rainy weather records the lowest average units sold.
* The variation between weather conditions is relatively small.
* Sales remain fairly stable across all weather conditions.

## Business Insights

* Weather may influence customer purchasing behavior.
* Customers appear slightly more active during sunny weather.
* Rainy conditions may reduce customer activity and demand.
* Weather information can be incorporated into forecasting models to improve prediction accuracy.

## Conclusion

Weather conditions show a moderate impact on sales performance. Including weather as a forecasting feature may improve demand prediction and inventory planning.
