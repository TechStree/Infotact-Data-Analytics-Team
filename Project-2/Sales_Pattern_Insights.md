# Sales Pattern Insights

## Overview

This report summarizes the major sales patterns identified during exploratory data analysis.

## Category Performance

* Furniture records the highest average sales.
* Electronics records the lowest average sales.
* Category-wise sales differences are small.

## Regional Performance

* South region records the highest average sales.
* West region records the lowest average sales.
* Regional demand remains balanced.

## Weather Impact

* Sunny weather records the highest average sales.
* Rainy weather records the lowest average sales.
* Weather appears to have a modest impact on demand.

## Seasonal Impact

* Autumn records the highest average sales.
* Summer records the lowest average sales.
* Seasonal demand patterns are present within the dataset.

## Key Business Takeaways

* Demand is relatively balanced across categories and regions.
* Weather and seasonality appear to influence sales performance.
* Inventory planning should consider seasonal and weather-related demand changes.
* Multiple variables should be included in forecasting models.

## Conclusion

The dataset demonstrates stable sales behavior while still showing meaningful seasonal and environmental patterns that can support demand forecasting and anomaly detection.
