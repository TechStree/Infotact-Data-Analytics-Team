# Dashboard Development Report

## Objective

The objective of this phase was to begin the development of the Supply Chain Analytics dashboard by preparing demand visualization components.

## Tasks Performed

* Loaded supply chain dataset.
* Converted Date column to datetime format.
* Aggregated Units Sold at daily level.
* Generated historical demand trend visualization.
* Verified dataset structure and dimensions.
* Examined demand patterns across the available timeline.

## Findings

* Dataset contains 73,100 records and 15 columns.
* Daily demand fluctuates significantly between approximately 11,000 and 17,000 units.
* Demand remains relatively stable over the two-year period with periodic spikes and drops.
* Historical demand trend can be effectively used for dashboard visualization.
