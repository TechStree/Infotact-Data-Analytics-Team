# Weather Analysis

## Weather Condition Distribution

| Weather Condition | Count |
| ----------------- | ----: |
| Sunny             | 18290 |

## Findings

* Sunny weather has the highest number of records with 18,290 entries.
* Cloudy weather has the lowest number of records with 18,260 entries.
* The difference between weather conditions is very small.
* All weather categories are evenly represented in the dataset.

## Business Insights

* Weather can significantly influence customer purchasing behavior.
* Certain products may experience higher demand during specific weather conditions.
* The balanced weather distribution allows reliable weather-based demand analysis.
* Weather condition can be used as a predictive feature in future forecasting models.
