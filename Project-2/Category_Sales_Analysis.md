# Category Sales Analysis

## Average Units Sold by Category

| Category    | Average Units Sold |
| ----------- | -----------------: |
| Furniture   |             137.77 |
| Groceries   |             136.92 |
| Clothing    |             136.69 |
| Toys        |             135.93 |
| Electronics |             135.01 |

## Findings

* Furniture records the highest average units sold.
* Electronics records the lowest average units sold.
* Sales volumes are relatively similar across all categories.
* No category dominates the sales distribution.

## Business Insights

* Customer demand appears balanced across product categories.
* Inventory planning should consider all categories rather than focusing heavily on one category.
* Category alone may not be a strong predictor of demand.
* Other factors such as promotions, seasonality, pricing, and weather may contribute more to demand variations.

## Conclusion

Product categories show very similar average sales performance. This indicates a balanced retail environment and suggests that demand forecasting should incorporate multiple business variables.
