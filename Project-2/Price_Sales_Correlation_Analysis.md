# Price and Sales Correlation Analysis

## Correlation Result

Correlation between Price and Units Sold:

0.0011

## Interpretation

* The correlation value is very close to zero.
* There is almost no linear relationship between product price and units sold.
* Changes in product price do not appear to significantly affect sales volume within this dataset.

## Business Insights

* Price alone is not a strong predictor of sales performance.
* Other business factors may have a greater impact on customer purchasing behavior.
* Variables such as promotions, seasonality, inventory availability, weather conditions, and product categories should be considered during forecasting.
* Demand forecasting models should include multiple features rather than relying on price alone.

## Conclusion

Price and Units Sold show almost no correlation in this dataset. This suggests that pricing is not the primary driver of sales variations and that additional business factors influence customer demand.
