# Inventory vs Units Sold Analysis

## Statistical Summary

### Inventory Level

* Mean: 274.47
* Minimum: 50
* Maximum: 500

### Units Sold

* Mean: 136.46
* Minimum: 0
* Maximum: 499

## Findings

* The average inventory level is approximately 274 units.
* The average number of units sold is approximately 136 units.
* Inventory levels are generally higher than sales volumes.
* Some products recorded zero sales despite being stocked.
* Certain products achieved very high sales volumes close to the maximum inventory levels.

## Business Insights

* Maintaining inventory levels higher than expected sales helps reduce stockout risk.
* Products with zero sales may require further investigation.
* High-selling products should be monitored carefully to ensure adequate inventory availability.
* Inventory management plays a critical role in meeting customer demand efficiently.
* This relationship will be useful during demand forecasting and anomaly detection phases.
