## Key Findings

* Inventory and sales show a moderate positive relationship.
* Price and discount have very little correlation with sales.
* Sales are balanced across categories and regions.
* Weather and seasonality show moderate influence on demand.
* Demand Forecast contains some negative values that require investigation.
