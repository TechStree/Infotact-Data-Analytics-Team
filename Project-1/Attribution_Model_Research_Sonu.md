# Attribution Model Research

## First Touch Attribution

First Touch Attribution assigns 100% conversion credit to the first marketing channel that introduced the customer.

### Example

Facebook Ad → Website Visit → Google Search → Purchase

Credit:

* Facebook Ad = 100%

### Advantages

* Identifies awareness channels
* Easy to understand

### Disadvantages

* Ignores later interactions

---

## Last Touch Attribution

Last Touch Attribution assigns 100% conversion credit to the final touchpoint before conversion.

### Example

Facebook Ad → Email → Google Search → Purchase

Credit:

* Google Search = 100%

### Advantages

* Simple implementation
* Easy reporting

### Disadvantages

* Ignores earlier touchpoints

---

## Linear Attribution

Linear Attribution distributes conversion credit equally across all touchpoints.

### Example

Facebook → Email → Google Search → Purchase

Credit:

* Facebook = 33.3%
* Email = 33.3%
* Google Search = 33.3%

### Advantages

* Considers the complete customer journey
* Fair credit distribution

### Disadvantages

* Assumes all touchpoints are equally important

---

## Attribution Model Comparison

| Model       | Credit Distribution | Advantage                 | Disadvantage                         |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| First Touch | First Channel       | Finds awareness source    | Ignores later interactions           |
| Last Touch  | Last Channel        | Easy to implement         | Ignores earlier interactions         |
| Linear      | Equal Distribution  | Considers all touchpoints | Equal weight may not reflect reality |
