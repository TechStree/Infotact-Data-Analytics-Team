# ROI Metrics Research

## Conversion Rate

Percentage of users who completed the desired action.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Cost incurred to acquire a customer.

## Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

Average cost required to generate one conversion.

## Return on Investment (ROI)

Measures profitability of marketing activities.

## Revenue Per Campaign

Revenue generated by each marketing campaign.

## Purpose

These metrics help evaluate campaign effectiveness and marketing performance.
