# Executive Summary

## Project Overview

The objective of this project was to analyze a marketing attribution dataset and identify the effectiveness of different marketing channels and campaigns in driving customer conversions.

## Dataset Summary

- Total Records: 10,000
- Total Columns: 5
- Marketing Channels: 6
- Marketing Campaigns: 6

## Key Findings

- Overall Conversion Rate: 49.44%
- Best Performing Channel: Email (50.18%)
- Lowest Performing Channel: Search Ads (47.98%)
- Best Performing Campaign: Brand Awareness (50.65%)
- Lowest Performing Campaign: New Product Launch (47.74%)

## Business Insights

- Email marketing generated the highest conversion rate among all channels.
- Brand Awareness campaigns achieved the strongest conversion performance.
- Conversion rates across channels were relatively balanced.
- Search Ads and New Product Launch campaigns may require optimization.

## Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing channels such as Email.
- Expand successful Brand Awareness campaigns.
- Review underperforming campaigns and channels.
- Continue monitoring attribution metrics for future marketing decisions.

## Conclusion

The marketing attribution analysis identified key channels and campaigns contributing to customer conversions. The findings provide valuable insights for improving marketing effectiveness and optimizing future campaigns.
