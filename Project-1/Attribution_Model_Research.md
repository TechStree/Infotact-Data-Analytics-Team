# Marketing Attribution Models

## First Touch Attribution

Credits the first marketing channel that introduced the customer.

## Last Touch Attribution

Credits the final marketing channel before conversion.

## Linear Attribution

Distributes credit equally across all touchpoints.

## Time Decay Attribution

Gives more credit to recent interactions before conversion.

## Position-Based Attribution

Assigns higher credit to the first and last touchpoints.

## Recommended Approach

For this project, Linear Attribution is a suitable starting point because it distributes credit evenly across marketing touchpoints.
