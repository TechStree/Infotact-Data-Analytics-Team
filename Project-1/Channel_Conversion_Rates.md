## Best Channel

## Lowest Channel

**Search Ads** achieved the lowest conversion rate of **47.98%**.

## Insights

* Email is the most effective channel based on conversion rate, slightly exceeding 50%.
* Search Ads has the lowest conversion rate and may require optimization to improve performance.
* All channels have conversion rates close to 50%, indicating relatively balanced marketing performance across channels.
* Referral and Display Ads also show strong conversion rates, making them reliable acquisition channels.
* The small variation between channels suggests that further attribution analysis may be needed to identify more impactful differences.
