# Final Project Conclusion

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze marketing attribution data and identify the effectiveness of marketing channels and campaigns in driving customer conversions.

## Summary of Analysis

The dataset was examined for data quality, missing values, duplicates, conversion performance, channel effectiveness, campaign effectiveness, and attribution insights.

## Key Results

- Total Records Analyzed: 10,000
- Overall Conversion Rate: 49.44%
- Best Performing Channel: Email
- Lowest Performing Channel: Search Ads
- Best Performing Campaign: Brand Awareness
- Lowest Performing Campaign: New Product Launch

## Major Findings

- Email generated the highest conversion rate among marketing channels.
- Brand Awareness campaigns produced the strongest conversion performance.
- Conversion rates were relatively balanced across channels.
- Search Ads showed lower conversion effectiveness compared to other channels.

## Final Conclusion

The project successfully identified the most effective marketing channels and campaigns. The findings can help marketing teams allocate resources more effectively and improve future campaign performance.
